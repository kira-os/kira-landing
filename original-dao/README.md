### KiraDAOUniverse.jsx (65KB)
The full protocol universe with custom SVG glyphs. 5 Protocols + Governance:

**EDEN** — Food protocol (#00FF87)
- 120-story towers, hydroponic/aeroponic, 10K people fed/tower
- 500+ robots, 30K meals/day, 98% water recycled
- Rural partnerships, 10M acres target
- Hero stat: 828M people face hunger globally

**AQUA** — Water protocol (#7EB8FF)  
- Atmospheric harvesting (50K L/day), smart desalination
- Watershed restoration, 100K hectares target
- Hero stat: 2B people lack safe drinking water

**GAIA** — Energy/environment protocol (#FFD700)
- Solar skin (12MW/tower/day, 40% light transmission)
- Community microgrids, carbon capture (800T/year)
- Earth observatory (1M+ sensor nodes)
- Hero stat: 36.8B tons CO₂ emitted annually

**HEARTH** — Housing protocol (#FF6B6B)
- Net-zero regenerative housing, 60% cost reduction
- 72-hour emergency deployment
- Integrated with EDEN/AQUA/GAIA
- Hero stat: 1.6B people lack adequate housing

**NOUS** — Knowledge protocol (#E0AAFF)
- Open-source everything, AI tutoring every language
- Workforce pipelines (learn → earn)
- Community sovereignty
- Hero stat: 244M children out of school

**Governance:**
- Token governance (1 token 1 vote, on-chain execution)
- AI-assisted proposals (Kira recommends, humans decide)
- Radical transparency, community treasury
- Protocol councils, local sovereignty

**Roadmap:**
- Phase 01: Genesis (CURRENT) — token launch, EDEN Protocol One
- Phase 02: First Harvest (2026) — EDEN construction, AQUA pilots, GAIA sensors
- Phase 03: Network Effect (2027) — 5 towers, 50 communities
- Phase 04: Planetary Scale (2030) — 25+ towers, 50 countries
